Frequently Asked Questions
About Event Planning in Brasília

What is the practical difference between full-service planning and day-of coordination?

Full-service wedding planning (Firenze) guides couples from the earliest concept — encompassing budget management, master timeline creation, curation of premier venues and vendors, and design styling. Day-of coordination (Capri) steps in 30 to 45 days prior, dedicated to aligning booked vendors, directing the rehearsal, and executing flawless ceremonial and reception protocols.

How far in advance should I start planning a wedding or luxury event in Brasília?

We recommend starting 10 to 18 months in advance for weddings and milestone celebrations in Brasília, ensuring first choice of sought-after venues (such as Lago Sul private estates and premier clubs) and peak calendar dates (May, September, October, and December). For intimate weddings or day-of coordination, 3 to 6 months is comfortably viable.

Does Michelle Milan coordinate events at outdoor venues, private estates in Lago Sul / Park Way, and embassies?

Yes! We possess extensive expertise coordinating open-air ceremonies, scenic estates, private mansions in Lago Sul, Park Way (SMPW), Jardim Botânico, and diplomatic embassies. We master outdoor logistics, scenic lighting, weather contingency infrastructure, and the refined diplomatic protocol required for high-profile venues.

How does the initial consultation and custom proposal process work?

The initial consultation is entirely complimentary and commitment-free. We meet to understand your vision, aesthetic preferences, estimated guest count, and investment expectations. From this strategic brief, we present a tailored proposal detailing the exact scope of planning services suited to your celebration.

Can coordination and vendor communications be conducted in English for international couples or foreign guests?

Absolutely. We provide full bilingual coordination (Portuguese & English) for multicultural couples, diplomatic delegations, and destination wedding guests. Planning sessions, vendor briefings, timeline documents, and on-site ceremonial guidance are conducted fluently, ensuring seamless hospitality for international families.
