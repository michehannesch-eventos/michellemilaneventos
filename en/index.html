Frequently Asked Questions
About Event Planning & Coordination

What is the practical difference between full-service planning and final coordination?

Full-service planning (Firenze for corporate and Venezia for social) accompanies you from the initial concept — encompassing budget guidance, vendor sourcing, venue visits, and end-to-end coordination. Day-of or final coordination (Capri and Milano) takes place in the final stretch, aligning contracted vendors, managing guest confirmations (R.S.V.P.), and directing on-site schedule and logistics so you can celebrate without stress.

How far in advance should I start planning my social or corporate event in Brasília?

For full-service planning, starting 6 to 12 months in advance is recommended to ensure ideal dates in popular banquet halls, farm estates, and party spaces. For smaller celebrations, corporate meetings, or final-phase coordination, we can readily assist with shorter timelines (2 to 4 months).

Does Michelle Milan coordinate events across different venues in Brasília and the Federal District?

Yes! We work across banquet halls, farm estates (chácaras), private clubs, company spaces, and private residences throughout Brasília, Plano Piloto, Lago Sul, Lago Norte, Park Way, Jardim Botânico, Águas Claras, and all administrative regions in the DF.

How does the initial consultation and budget proposal work?

The initial consultation is completely commitment-free. We listen to your ideas, event style, guest count, and budget expectations. Based on this conversation, we prepare a transparent proposal tailored to the reality and goals of your event.

Can coordination and vendor communications be conducted in English for international guests or teams?

Yes. We offer bilingual support (Portuguese & English) to welcome foreign guests, international families, and multinational corporate teams with complete clarity and hospitality.
